An accelerometer worn on the wrist or the waist records how somebody moves, and a classifier reads
windows of that signal to say whether they were walking, climbing stairs or sitting still. In a
controlled recording the battery is not a constraint. A long-term study in somebody's own house is
where the experiment outlasts the battery and blocks of data go missing. Sampling faster drains it,
because a sensor's energy consumption scales with the data it produces. Longer windows are the
obvious escape, and they cost delayed predictions and windows that straddle two activities.

Context reaches a classifier by three routes rather than one. A higher sampling rate and a wider
window are two of them, and the third is a model over the sequence of predictions. We chain a
conditional random field onto each classifier's output, with its class probabilities as the node
potentials. The sampling rate is set on the device before anything is recorded, and the field runs on
the predictions once everything has been.

We ran roughly 1300 configurations over three public datasets, sampling rates from 5 to 50 Hz and
window lengths from 1.5 to 6 seconds, with no participant in both training and test folds. On the
smartphone dataset, logistic regression over statistical features climbs from 0.85 at the sparsest
corner to about 0.94. It arrives there at 3 second windows and 20 Hz, and 6 seconds at 50 Hz gets no
further on five times the samples. Chaining the field lifted the weakest configuration by about nine
points to over 90 percent without touching the sensor, though one of the three datasets gains almost
nothing from it. The full grids and the CNN and LSTM comparison are in the paper.

All of this rests on laboratory recordings. Somebody in them walks a staircase for minutes at a time,
and hardly anybody does that at home. Where the plateau sits once activities run that short and that
interleaved is the question, and no public dataset can answer it. The ceiling is probably lower,
because a window carries context only while one activity outlasts it, which is conjecture until
somebody collects that data.
