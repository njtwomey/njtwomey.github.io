Activity recognition in a house full of sensors decides, second by second, whether the person moving around it
is walking, sitting or standing up out of a chair. A minute of steady walking is easy to annotate, and the
exact second at which sitting becomes standing is where two careful annotators will disagree. Nine of the
twenty labels here are transitions, so a vote handing the entrant one integer per second makes a second
everybody agreed on indistinguishable from a second they split three ways.

This challenge releases the disagreement rather than resolving it. The target for each second is the
proportion of annotators who assigned that second each of the twenty labels, and a submission is scored
against those proportions by a weighted Brier score. The class weights are raised on the harder classes when
the unscripted recordings join the scripted ones. Confidence is rewarded in the middle of a long walk and
penalised on the second somebody stands up, so an entrant cannot win by being decisive.

Twelve annotators worked in ELAN from 4K head-camera footage that is not part of the release. Raw video from
the three RGB-D cameras is withheld as well, to preserve the participants' anonymity, so vision reaches an
entrant as bounding box corners in pixels and a three-dimensional centre of mass in millimetres. The floor
plan, the wrist accelerometer, the infra-red sensors and the individual annotator files are in the paper.

A proportion is only as fine as the number of annotators behind it. The record the paper plots as an example
was labelled by two of the twelve, which permits a target of nought, a half or one and nothing in between. How
many annotators a soft label needs before it measures ambiguity rather than who was on shift is the open
question. The disagreement presumably concentrates in the transitions, so extra annotators there and fewer
elsewhere would sharpen the targets where they are split. That is conjecture from a document which sets the
rules and reports no experiments, though the annotator files it releases would settle it.
